Transient pilot study and warm-up validation for the MeshSorter simulation
=======================================================================

PART 1. Welch-style pilot: when does the expected throughput settle?
-------------------------------------------------------------------
300 independent runs per configuration from the empty state, throughput
averaged over blocks of 20 time steps, then averaged across the 300 runs.
'settle' is the last block at which the ensemble average departs from its
long-run level by more than 5.5 standard errors (and by more than 3e-4).
A(w) is the cumulative excess from time w onward, in item-steps: a run that
deletes w steps and then averages over T steps carries a bias of A(w)/T.
The pilot resolves A only to about +-100 items.
